The machine that prints on your wall

It stands in front of your wall and moves along a rail on the floor. The print head goes up and down and prints the picture strip by strip.

  • Prints on painted walls, plaster and concrete
  • Full colour, sharp even on big walls
  • A touch screen shows your design before we start

How it works

  1. Send your idea

    Share a photo, a design or just a rough idea on WhatsApp.

  2. We measure the wall

    We visit, check the surface and give you a fixed price.

  3. Preview before printing

    You see the design at wall size and approve it.

  4. Printed in one day

    The machine prints directly on the wall and the ink dries the same day.
